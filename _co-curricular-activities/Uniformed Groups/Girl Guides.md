<h3>CCA Objectives</h3>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To provide the girls with the opportunity for self-training in the development
of character, in the realisation of responsible citizenship and promotion
of service to the community.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To maintain a high ethical standard based on the aims and principles embodied
in the "Promise and Law" as laid down by the Founder.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To encourage international goodwill and understanding by establishing
friendly relations with girls of other nations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Overall to provide unparalleled opportunities for girls to discover and
develop their strengths, their skills and their fullest potential, to become
responsible citizens of the world.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Key programmes</h3>
<p>Motto :&nbsp;<strong>Be Prepared</strong>&nbsp;A comprehensive 5-Point
Programme enables Girl Guides to develop in 5 areas:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Personal &amp; Social Development</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Home</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Outdoor</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Community</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>International</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The programmes craft inline to the 5-Point Programme teaches girls vital
life skills – such as personal goal-setting, decision-making and team-building
– in a safe and supportive environment.</p>
<p>Leadership camps for&nbsp; Company Leaders, Patrol Leaders, Camp training
, Girl conference 2018 ,&amp; other&nbsp; school -based leadership camps.</p>
<p>Training sessions: Garden By the Bay Ambassador Programme, GGS Singapore
Cancer Society -Smoke Free Ambassador, GGS TTSH Care for the Elderly will
be when guides enjoy working for interest badges and awards that will help
them develop new skills as well as being prepared to help others through
meaningful community service, such as caring for the elderly to volunteering
to help the less privileged!</p>
<p>GGS WAGGGS Surf Smart Badge Programme : Guiding creates many opportunities
for girls to understand complex issues confronting the world today – such
as cyber safety, anti-bullying and environmental sustainability.</p>
<p>Other Proficiency and Interest Badge Programes: More importantly, Guiding
provides hands-on activities that equip them with skills to take action
by leading and participating in community service projects that make an
impact on the community.</p>
