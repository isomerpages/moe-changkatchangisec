<h2>60<sup>th</sup> Anniversary Show @ OTH</h2>
<p>On 5 June 2026, our Changkat Changi Secondary School proudly celebrated
its 60<sup>th</sup> Anniversary at Our Tampines Hub Festive Plaza, marking
six decades of excellence, growth and service to the community. The celebration
brought together students, teachers, parents, alumni and members of the
public for an evening of performances and interactive activities that showcased
the school's vibrant talents, rich traditions and strong community spirit.</p>

<p>The celebration began with a warm welcome from our confident student emcees,
who guided the audience through the programme with enthusiasm and professionalism.
The Concert Band opened the performances with an energetic selection of
musical pieces, setting a festive atmosphere for the evening. This was
followed by an impressive display from the Boys' Brigade, whose stirring
bagpipe and snare drum performance captivated the audience with its precision,
discipline and ceremonial flair.</p>

<p>The celebrations continued with performances by the Chinese String Ensemble,
whose elegant melodies reflected the school's appreciation for its cultural
heritage. The audience was then treated to lively and colourful performances
by the Malay Dance and Modern Dance groups; each demonstrating the students'
creativity, dedication and countless hours of practice. Every performance
highlighted the diverse talents of our students and the commitment of teachers
and instructors who worked tirelessly behind the scenes to prepare them.</p>

<p>The celebration concluded with an engaging quiz segment that invited members
of the public to participate, thereby creating a lively and interactive
ending to the event. The active involvement of everyone within the space
of the Festive Plaza reflected the strong partnerships and school spirit
that have shaped the school's journey over the past 60 years.</p>
<p>The 60<sup>th</sup> Anniversary Celebration was a memorable occasion that
honoured the school's proud heritage while celebrating the achievements
of its community. The event reflected the values of teamwork, excellence
and unity that continue to guide the school today. As we commemorate this
important milestone, we look forward to building on our strong foundation
and creating many more meaningful memories together in the years ahead.</p>
