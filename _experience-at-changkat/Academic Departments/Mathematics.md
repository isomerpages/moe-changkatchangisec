<p><strong>Learning Outcomes:</strong>
</p>
<p>The Mathematics Department believes that every Changkateer can become
a confident and creative problem-solver. By integrating opportunities for
students to apply Computational Thinking (CT) in Mathematics, we help students
develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and digital literacy skills
— key competencies for the future.</p>
<p>Students are also exposed to Problems in Real-World Context (PRWC) questions
in examinations, which challenge them to apply higher-order thinking skills
to authentic real-life situations. Through these experiences, we aim to
nurture adaptable and innovative thinkers who are able to make meaningful
connections between Mathematics and the world around them.</p>
<p>Beyond the classroom, the department is committed to developing students’
talents and interests through our Talent Development Programme (TDP). Students
are given opportunities to participate in various Mathematics and coding
competitions, where they have attained commendations and awards at both
national and international levels.</p>
<p>To deepen students’ understanding of the relevance of Mathematics in the
workplace, learning journeys are also organised to organisations such as
SCOR and HSBC, where students gain insights into how Mathematics is applied
in the financial sector and beyond.</p>
